# Code of conduct

This applies to issues, pull requests and any other discussion in my repos.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep criticism about the work, not the person.
- Help newcomers; everyone started somewhere.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment or personal attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Sharing someone's private details without their permission.

## If something goes wrong

Tell me through <https://m51v5.com/contact>. I'll read every report and keep it private.

I may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block anyone who breaks these rules, and I'll say why when I can.
